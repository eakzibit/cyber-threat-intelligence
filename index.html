Reporting scope

This register loads the published data file when opened and refreshes it while the page is open. The embedded baseline is a fallback, not a live feed. Inclusion follows the date of a qualifying exploitation or remediation development, not just CVE disclosure. Initial backfill includes verified KEV additions and selected primary-source research. Date-only entries on 23 November are included provisionally because exact publication times are not available. The CISA site and some vendor pages were inaccessible; the official KEV mirror (catalogue 2026.11.25) was used. Coverage beyond that feed is best effort. Unknown scores, versions and EPSS are intentionally not guessed. No organisation-specific exposure is asserted.

Filter intelligence

Published updates are loaded from the shared repository. You may also import CTI JSON updates to merge by CVE locally; local imports do not publish to other viewers. Export the updated register to keep a portable backup. Browser storage is best effort and local to this browser.

Defensive intelligence only. Priority and response SLAs are suggested operational guidance, not regulatory deadlines. Confirm vendor patches and exposure before implementation. Email opens an editable draft; no mail is sent automatically.
