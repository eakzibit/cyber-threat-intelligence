Reporting scope

This register loads the published data file when opened and refreshes it while the page is open. The embedded baseline is a fallback, not a live feed. Inclusion follows the date of a qualifying exploitation or remediation development, not just CVE disclosure. Initial backfill and subsequent publications include verified KEV additions and selected primary-source research. Date-only entries on 23 November are included provisionally because exact publication times are not available. Coverage remains best effort and incomplete; publication time does not establish the latest successful check of every feed. Unknown scores, versions and EPSS are intentionally not guessed. No organisation-specific exposure is asserted.
